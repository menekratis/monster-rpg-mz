# HUSHWAKE — Quests

> **Document status:** Revised quest foundation using intelligent synthetic Wildkin, Tuner conflicts, public systems, and root-access clues. Field Data rules are canonical; exact reward values remain provisional.

## Quest design standard

A HUSHWAKE quest should do at least two of the following:

- reveal a wildkin personality, synthetic body function, or chosen relationship to an inherited service role;
- change a route, habitat, or public system;
- develop a recurring character;
- teach or deepen a system;
- provide a clue or conflicting interpretation;
- create a battle with a distinct tactical premise;
- visibly change the world after completion.

If a quest only asks the player to collect items for currency, it does not belong in the vertical slice.

### Structure rules

- Most optional quests contain 2–3 meaningful steps.
- Use no arbitrary kill counts.
- Reuse maps, but change the player’s interpretation or traversal.
- Objectives state the current lead in plain language.
- Critical story evidence never depends on optional completion.
- Optional clues strengthen or complicate a theory.
- No quest expires in the slice.
- No dialogue choice permanently blocks a species, technique, or story scene.
- When two solutions exist, both should express the same creature behavior from different angles rather than create an expensive branch.

## Main quest — The Missing Chorus

This is one quest with clear stages, not a stack of simultaneous mandatory errands.

### Stage 1: Wake Skein and clear the cable approach

**Player-facing lead:** “Complete the final body checks before the Hearthmere cable departs.”

- Move through the service bay and complete three short physical checks on the body the protagonist reconstructed.
- Let Skein examine its body and offer Accord; accept and optionally rename it.
- Notice the transient `ROUTE PEER: RETURNED` message and unplanned relay filament without receiving an explanation.
- Ride the cable car and complete the first 1v1 battle using a highlighted Assault tell.
- Observe the swarm leaving along the route it arrived from.

**Teaches:** Movement, protagonist repair identity, voluntary Accord, basic 1v1 command flow, Resolve, and Aspect/damage preview. The battle does not universally reveal the opponent's next action.

**Story result:** The player reaches Hearthmere with a companion that chose them; a beloved relay swarm repeats Skein’s impossible handshake while replaying an obsolete route.

### Stage 2: Find where the swarm turned back

**Player-facing lead:** “Survey the Sunken Track between Hearthmere and Bellwether Orchard.”

- Use Survey on the unavoidable fork.
- Enter or avoid one visible encounter group.
- Form the first Accord with an ordinary wildkin.
- Make one clearly prompted but tactically real switch during an authored visible wind-up: the already-committed action targets the incoming reserve. This cue teaches targeting without making prediction universal.
- Follow doubled tracks toward Lower Track.

**Teaches:** Visible 1v1 encounters, Survey, wild Open/Accord, action-cost switching, shared Focus, route fork.

**Story result:** The swarm reached the track, received a root-class handshake, then replayed its route backward. The broken-weir explanation becomes incomplete.

### Stage 3: Ask what the orchard heard

**Player-facing lead:** “Meet Edda at Bellwether Orchard and restore the two silent way shells.”

- See the closed blossoms and missing pollinators.
- Tune two open shell-relays using visible handshake spacing.
- Identify gray checksum-scale dust and an unregistered coordinator identifier as Orralume’s.

**Teaches:** Tune and habitat consequence.

**Story result:** Orralume is likely sheltering below the old weir, and the migration cannot stabilize without it.

### Stage 4: Recover the consent interval

**Player-facing lead:** “Inspect the Weirworks for Orralume and the missing response in the vent protocol.”

- Mara authorizes entry; Merrit holds the mobile Stillpoint at low power.
- Open a short route loop using Survey and Rig.
- Find the mandatory modern continuity protocol with its response interval removed.
- Use Edda’s remembered call to identify an open control plate; recover the answering half from a Vellumoth trace and restore the mutual handshake.

**Teaches:** Mixed exploration loop and story deduction.

**Story result:** Community-readable protocol and modern measurements together offer a way to vent pressure without forcing seedcore rollback.

### Stage 5: Decide who leads the operation

**Player-facing lead:** “Reach Tavi at the upper controls before the next surge.”

- Tavi commits to full Stillpoint rollback.
- Player presents the consent-interval evidence.
- Complete Tavi’s sequential 1v1 Tuner battle for operational lead.
- Recover fully at the control-platform waypost.

**Teaches:** Reading a coordinated Tuner lineup across reserves, voluntary versus free replacement, future cross-switch resource potential, immediate retry, and post-victory Field Data synchronization.

**Story result:** Tavi grants the player one attempt and becomes an active part of the combined solution. Every Wildkin in the player's selected battle lineup receives the same full base Field Data reward.

### Stage 6: Bring Orralume out of the Hush

**Player-facing lead:** “Break the forced authorization locks and give Orralume a route it can accept.”

- Complete the three-phase guardian encounter.
- Switch under at least one displayed incoming action if the chosen roster requires it; no particular species is mandatory.
- Use Tune during the visible inverted interval.
- Return via the cistern lift.

**Teaches:** Major-battle phases, reserve Resolve management, and Field actions in 1v1 combat.

**Story result:** Orralume rejects the rollback, accepts the open route, leads the Thrumble home, and restores the orchard mesh while the weir vents.

### Stage 7: Record the Hearthmere Return

**Player-facing lead:** “Meet Mara at the Return relay.”

- Walk through at least one visibly changed space.
- Receive the Hearthmere Return Waymark.
- Choose a Curious, Practical, or Empathetic emphasis for the official note.
- See Merrit’s private recorder-core stinger.

**Teaches:** Waymarks as story progression and small player expression.

**Story result:** The local arc closes; the Hush becomes a regional thread.

## Optional quest 1 — The Borrowed Key

### Availability

Starts from Pell in Hearthmere after the initial briefing. Remains available through the post-climax return.

### Hook

The Return relay’s physical authentication key is missing. Pell admits they made harmless practice tokens for local Latchlings, accidentally teaching one that the live key was part of their access game.

### Steps

1. **Inspect the relay and token trail.** Survey shows the Latchling carried the key toward the signal rise off Lower Track.
2. **Resolve the nest encounter.** The player may offer Pell’s bright practice credential during the encounter, or battle and form Accord normally. Both solutions use the species’ love of challenge-response patterns.
3. **Return the key.** Pell and Edda change its public pattern and redesign the practice game rather than blaming the wildkin.

### Rewards

- Permanent signal-rise shortcut into Bellwether Orchard.
- Latchling bestiary observation and early temperament hint.
- A small technique reward favoring Feint/Guard-breaking play.
- Optional clue: the key uses a Meridian root credential format and validation date older than Meridian’s official Hearthmere installation.

### Design purposes

- Teaches that field behavior can alter an Accord encounter.
- Develops Pell and Edda.
- Opens a useful route.
- Foreshadows Meridian activity without making the main plot depend on it.

### Post-climax variant

If completed after the main story, the Latchling has moved closer to the festival and the quest is shorter. The clue and technique remain available.

## Optional quest 2 — A Bed for Loamlet

### Availability

Begins by inspecting a collapsed habitat gap on Upper Track. Niko or Edda can provide a hint if the player passes it twice.

### Hook

A conduit-wall repair blocked the low soil-service passage used by Loamlets. The displaced group now prints seed mesh across the walking path and repeatedly collides with workers and courier carts.

### Steps

1. **Survey the repair.** The Wayglass reveals the old low passage and a nearby washed-out seed mat.
2. **Rig a safer gap.** Carry the single microbial seed mat from the drainage shelf and use the field kit to bridge power and brace a low opening; no multi-item fetch count.
3. **Observe the return.** A Loamlet scans the opening, chooses to print a living bed through it, and opens a shortcut between the wall loop and Hearthmere side of the track.

### Rewards

- Permanent route shortcut.
- Loamlet bestiary observation and temperament hint.
- One additional Field Kit charge for the rest of the slice, represented as a self-sealing microbial wrap learned from the Loamlet bed.
- Optional clue: the checksum ring in the displaced Loamlet’s shell pattern stops precisely at the Stillpoint handshake boundary.

### Design purposes

- Shows human repair causing an unintended habitat problem without villainy.
- Teaches Rig and environmental return states.
- Offers a meaningful quality-of-life reward.
- Gives the Hush a physical boundary.

### Scope protection

The restored gap is an authored two-state event, not a general habitat-building system.

## Optional quest 3 — Three Notes Missing

### Availability

Begins with Edda after tuning the orchard shells. Completes in the Weirworks archive before or after Orralume.

### Hook

Edda remembers the old vent protocol as a five-part call and response, but the surviving orchard surface records only the two-part call. The current Meridian protocol uses three silent root pulses—and the wildkin are not given a response window.

### Steps

1. **Compare the orchard sources.** Tune records the two visible call intervals and marks the missing response.
2. **Observe Vellumoth in the archive.** A wing molt has lifted faded pigment and data residue from an older maintenance surface. Surveying it recovers the responding pattern.
3. **Test the full handshake.** An optional open-protocol plate waits for a nearby wildkin to answer, opens a legacy cradle alcove, and confirms the consent interval before the final operation.

### Rewards

- Wider timing window for the Orralume Tune sequence, or an equivalent clear boss assistance if timing is not used.
- Vellumoth bestiary observation and temperament hint.
- Archive note showing that the response was intentionally removed from later standards as “operator-dependent” and replaced with a Meridian credential.
- A brief topology image linking the weir cradle to sites outside the known Merefold network.
- A post-climax dialogue exchange between Mara and Edda about making local practice testable and teachable.

### Design purposes

- Turns technical lore and synthetic creature behavior into a useful investigation.
- Integrates a species with the dungeon’s function.
- Rewards attention with mechanical help without making the boss impossible otherwise.
- Deepens the story’s tradition/standardization tension.

### Accessibility rule

The comparison uses pulse spacing, shape, and controller vibration where supported. Pitch recognition is never required.

## Quest log presentation

Each active quest shows:

- one-sentence current lead;
- relevant place name;
- optional “What we know” summary of no more than three bullets;
- no floating distance counter;
- no checklist of undiscovered bonus conditions.

When a clue updates the main quest, the new interpretation replaces the old lead rather than appending a long journal transcript.

Example:

> **The Missing Chorus**  
> Orralume’s checksum scales lead beneath the old weir. Mara is waiting at the service gate.  
> We know: the Thrumble received a root handshake before the pressure surge; the same signal reaches the orchard.

## Rewards philosophy

Priority order:

1. Changed route or habitat.
2. New tactical option.
3. Better understanding of a creature or mystery.
4. Refillable utility improvement.
5. Currency only if a meaningful economy exists.

No randomized reward tables. The player should understand why this quest produced this benefit. Qualifying battle Data is synchronized in full to every selected lineup member; it is never split by participation.

## Future quest variety

If the full project continues, local arcs should vary beyond investigation:

- mediate a transit route shared by workers, autonomous cargo wildkin, and an independent colony;
- prepare and enter a public team-and-transport contest whose outcome changes credential policy;
- escort a newly Rewoven industrial wildkin that refuses its assigned destination without battling it;
- choose which district receives a limited continuity system, then manage welfare and service consequences;
- reconstruct a cradle route from conflicting human, institutional, and seedcore-carried records;
- help a rival solve a problem where their method is better.

This is a variety prompt, not a full-game quest list.

## Quest review template

Before approving a quest, record:

- **Player verb:** What does the player actually do?
- **New decision:** What choice is not present in the main path?
- **Character/ecology change:** What is visibly different afterward?
- **Reused content:** Which existing map, species, and system carry it?
- **Reward:** Why does the result logically grant it?
- **Critical dependency:** What must still be understandable if skipped?
- **Cut test:** What is lost if the quest is removed?

If the cut test has no strong answer, cut it.

## Quest playtest questions

1. Do players notice at least one optional quest without map-marker clutter?
2. Can they explain why each route changes after completion?
3. Does either Borrowed Key solution feel like the “wrong” one?
4. Is the Loamlet Field Kit reward strong enough to feel useful but not mandatory?
5. Do players connect Three Notes Missing to the boss Tune without a character restating it?
6. Are post-climax quest variants worth keeping, or do they add fragile state for little value?
7. Does each side quest show a different daily use of synthetic wildkin without reducing them to tools?
8. Do the key, checksum rings, and Vellumoth trace build one coherent root-access mystery?

## Decisions awaiting approval

- One staged main quest rather than many mandatory log entries.
- Exactly three slice side quests.
- No kill-count or multi-item collection padding.
- Every side quest changes a route, public system, battle option, or interpretation of synthetic life.
- Optional quests remain completable after the climax with compact state variants.
- Tavi's confrontation is a Tuner battle; ordinary autonomous opponents use Wild Encounters.
- Qualifying victories grant the full base Field Data reward to every Wildkin in the selected battle lineup.
- Wildkin may speak during quest scenes when personality and context warrant it, without turning every wild encounter into dialogue.
